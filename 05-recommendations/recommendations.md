# Business Recommendations – PayNova

### 1. Strengthen Fraud Controls (High Priority)
- Implement stricter rules or step-up authentication for high-value transactions (especially > 100k).
- Add extra scrutiny on Credit Card and Wallet transactions and on the Web channel.
- Create a watchlist for merchants and customers with repeated fraud involvement.

### 2. Improve Success Rates
- Investigate and fix root causes of failures on **Bank Transfer** and **API** channel.
- Work with banks on reducing “Network Error” and “Timeout” failures.
- Improve real-time balance checks to reduce “Insufficient Funds” declines where possible.

### 3. Channel Strategy
- Continue investing in **Mobile App** (highest volume + better control).
- Improve Web channel security and monitoring (higher fraud exposure).
- Review API partner integrations that show lower success rates.

### 4. Merchant Risk Management
- Introduce differentiated risk scoring for merchants.
- Apply higher reserves or delayed settlement for high-fraud-rate merchants.
- Provide fraud prevention tools/education to merchants in high-risk categories.

### 5. Customer Value Management
- Identify and nurture top customers by successful volume (loyalty / better limits / dedicated support).
- Flag customers with repeated fraud or abnormal failure patterns for review.

### 6. Monitoring & Reporting
- Build a weekly/monthly dashboard tracking:
  - Success Rate
  - Fraud Rate & Fraud Loss
  - Failure reasons trend
  - Top risky merchants
- Set alert thresholds (e.g., fraud rate > X% on any method/channel).

---

These recommendations turn SQL findings into concrete actions a Fintech Business Analyst or Product/Risk team can own.
