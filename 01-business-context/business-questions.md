# Business Questions & KPIs – PayNova Payments

### Main Business Questions

1. **Overall Performance**  
   What is the total transaction volume, value, success rate, and fraud rate?

2. **Channel & Payment Method Performance**  
   Which payment methods and channels have the highest success rates and volume?

3. **Fraud Analysis**  
   Where is fraud concentrated (payment method, amount range, time, merchant)?  
   What is the financial impact of fraud?

4. **Customer Analysis**  
   Who are the highest-value customers?  
   Are there customers with unusually high fraud or failure rates?

5. **Merchant Analysis**  
   Which merchants generate the most volume and revenue?  
   Which merchants have elevated fraud or failure rates?

6. **Time Trends**  
   How do volume, success rate, and fraud rate change over time (daily/monthly)?

7. **Failed Transactions**  
   What are the main reasons for failure?  
   Which methods/channels fail most often?

8. **Revenue & Fees**  
   How much fee revenue is generated?  
   Which segments contribute most to revenue?

---

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| Total Transaction Value | Sum of successful transaction amounts |
| Success Rate % | Successful transactions / Total transactions |
| Fraud Rate % | Fraudulent transactions / Total transactions |
| Fraud Loss | Sum of amounts on fraudulent transactions |
| Average Transaction Value | Total value / Number of transactions |
| Fee Revenue | Sum of fees collected |
| Top Channel by Volume | Channel with highest number of transactions |
| High-Risk Merchant Count | Merchants with fraud rate above threshold |
