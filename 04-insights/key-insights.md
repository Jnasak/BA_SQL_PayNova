# Key Insights – PayNova Payments Analysis

### 1. Overall Health
- Success rate is generally strong (around 89–94%), but there is still room for improvement.
- Fraud rate is low in percentage terms but creates meaningful financial loss, especially on higher-value transactions.

### 2. Payment Method Insights
- **UPI** usually drives the highest volume and has competitive success rates.
- **Credit Card** and **Wallet** tend to show higher fraud rates.
- **Bank Transfer** often has a higher failure rate (network / bank-side issues).

### 3. Channel Insights
- **Mobile App** is the dominant channel by volume.
- **Web** channel often shows elevated fraud rates compared to Mobile App and POS.
- API channel can have lower success rates depending on integration quality.

### 4. Fraud Patterns
- Fraud concentration increases significantly on **high-value transactions** (> 100k).
- Certain merchant categories and individual merchants contribute disproportionately to fraud loss.
- A small number of customers appear repeatedly in fraudulent transactions.

### 5. Failed Transactions
- Top failure reasons are typically: Insufficient Funds, Card Declined, Network Error, and Timeout.
- Failure rates vary clearly by payment method and channel — this is actionable.

### 6. Customer & Merchant Concentration
- A relatively small group of customers and merchants drive a large share of successful volume and fee revenue (Pareto pattern).
- High-value merchants with elevated fraud rates need closer monitoring or stricter controls.
